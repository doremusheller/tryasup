The basics.

What is Sup?

Sup is a hemp mocktail beverage made for alcohol-free social sipping. It is designed to offer cocktail-style flavor and a relaxed beverage experience without alcohol.

Does Sup contain alcohol?

No. Sup is an alcohol-free beverage.

What does “hemp mocktail” mean?

It means Sup is inspired by mocktail flavors and made with hemp-derived ingredients. It is intended for adults looking for a modern alternative to alcoholic drinks.

How should I drink Sup?

Serve chilled, pour over ice, or dress it up with citrus, herbs, fruit, or a cocktail garnish. It works straight from the can or as a base for alcohol-free mixed drinks.

Effects & experience.

How will Sup make me feel?

Everyone’s experience may vary. Sup is made for relaxed, social moments, but individual effects depend on your body, tolerance, serving size, and other factors.

How long does it take to feel anything?

Timing can vary by person. Start with one serving, give yourself time, and avoid consuming more until you understand how it feels for you.

Can I drive after drinking Sup?

Do not drive or operate machinery if you feel impaired. Use responsibly and follow all applicable laws.

Who should not drink Sup?

Sup is intended for adults. Do not consume if you are pregnant, nursing, underage, or have been advised by a medical professional to avoid hemp-derived products.
