Legal Disclaimer

Sup products are intended for responsible consumption by adults aged 21 and over. By entering and using this website, you confirm that you are of legal age to consume hemp-derived products in your jurisdiction.

Sup products contain hemp-derived cannabinoids and are not intended for use by individuals under the age of 21, pregnant or nursing women, or anyone operating heavy machinery or vehicles.

Always consult a healthcare professional before using cannabinoid-based products, especially if you have any pre-existing medical conditions or are taking medications.

Please be aware of and comply with all applicable local and state laws regarding the purchase, possession, and consumption of hemp-derived beverages. Laws and regulations may vary between states, and it is the consumer’s responsibility to know and follow the rules that apply to them.

Sup assumes no responsibility for improper use, overconsumption, or use by underage individuals.

Always store Sup products in a secure location, out of reach of children and pets.
